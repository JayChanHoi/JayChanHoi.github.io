About Me

I'm Jay Chan, an AI engineering lead and solution architect based in Hong Kong. I have 8+ years taking machine learning and generative AI into production, including in regulated financial services.

At the Hong Kong Monetary Authority I led AI CoE delivery and governance: multi-agent platforms, RAG systems, and quality monitoring, including an organisation-wide meeting-minutes solution. Before that I was AI solution architect and product lead for governed GenAI programmes at the Hong Kong Productivity Council, and I built production recommendation, deep-RL trading, and LLM products in industry.

I am a founder of Oraclens Intelligence, where I build agent-native tools for research and investment analysis.

What I care about is last-mile delivery: framing the workflow, evaluating the system, and getting people to actually use it. My longer-running interests are reinforcement learning, NLP, and quantitative trading.

Outside work I coach Taekwondo, train in the gym, and follow new AI research.
