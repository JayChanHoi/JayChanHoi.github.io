Delivery is the point.

I'm Jay Chan. I build AI systems that people actually use, and I keep working until the workflow changes around them.

At the Hong Kong Monetary Authority I led AI CoE delivery: multi-agent platforms, retrieval, and quality monitoring, including an organisation-wide meeting-minutes solution. Before that I led governed GenAI programmes at the Hong Kong Productivity Council, and I shipped recommendation systems and deep-RL trading systems in industry.

Now I'm building Oraclens Intelligence — oraclens.ai — an agent research desk for retail investors and traders: research briefs, monitoring, portfolio intelligence, and agent-written strategy notes. The work I keep returning to is agent systems: a skill, a loop, and a research note that holds up once someone actually uses it.

Outside work I coach Taekwondo, train, and follow new AI research.
