About Me

I'm Jay Chan, founder of Oraclens Intelligence and an AI engineering lead in Hong Kong. For more than eight years I have taken machine learning into production — recommendation, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and governed generative AI.

At the Hong Kong Monetary Authority I led AI CoE delivery: multi-agent platforms, retrieval, and quality monitoring, including an organisation-wide meeting-minutes solution. Before that I led governed GenAI programmes at the Hong Kong Productivity Council, and I shipped recommendation systems and deep-RL trading systems in industry.

At Oraclens I build agent-native tools for research and investment analysis. The work I keep returning to is reinforcement learning for trading, news-and-price models, and computer vision on real operations.

I care about last-mile delivery: frame the workflow, evaluate the system, and get people to use it. Outside work I coach Taekwondo, train, and follow new AI research.
